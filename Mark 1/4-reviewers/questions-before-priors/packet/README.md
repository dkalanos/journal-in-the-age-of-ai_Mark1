# Reviewer packet · questions-before-priors, round 1

You are asked one question: is what this paper adds of interest to the community this journal serves, under `interest-criteria.md`?

- `paper/` is the paper, written for people; `appendix/` is the appendix, for the reader who checks.
- `findings.md` is the machine's findings report from journal run `20261028-233241-journal-047023d`: form, correspondence and warrant, each finding with a pointer into the record and the standard.
- `evidence-map.md` places the contribution claim against the corpus. An editor has already signed what is new, on this map.

You are not asked to redo Levels 1 to 3. A review round is a question the author can answer within the existing record: a clarification, a reframed claim, an analysis on materials already there. A request that needs new materials is new work and a new submission.

If you sign that the paper is of interest, your name goes on the certificate. A judgment that it is not produces no public object.
