# Interest criteria · version 0.1

Published object 5 (appendix H). The criteria by which this community judges whether what is new in a paper is
of interest. The editor and two named reviewers sign this judgment; it is the one judgment no machine input
decides. Demonstration text: each journal writes its own, in its own words, and this is where journals differ.

A paper that a signed editor has judged new is of interest to this community when at least one holds:

1. **It changes what a reader in the field would predict or do.** A result that reverses, bounds or sharpens
   an expectation the field acts on.
2. **It connects streams that do not read each other.** A result that a reader in one stream needs and would
   not otherwise find.
3. **It makes a question answerable.** A method, construct or formal device that lets others ask something
   they could not ask before.
4. **It settles a live disagreement.** A result that bears directly on a question the community is divided on.

Reviewers judge interest only. They are not asked whether the paper is correct, well formed or new: the
machine's findings, the editor's intake decision and the editor's "new" signature have answered those. A
judgment of "not of interest" produces no public object.
