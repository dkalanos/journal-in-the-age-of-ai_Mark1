# Definitions of record

- **elementary causal mechanism** — `theta: A -> Delta(X)`. An element of the finite vocabulary Theta, known to all agents from the outset: a map from action profiles to distributions over consequences, the reduced form of whatever detailed causal model underlies the state-action-consequence relation. Not to be confused with: A theory (an assignment of mechanisms to awareness cells); the paper imposes no structural-causal-model formalism, and expansion of the mechanism vocabulary itself is outside the paper.
- **objective assignment (objective causal system)** — `tau: S -> Theta; x ~ tau(s)(a)`. The primitive tau assigning a mechanism to each state; tau(s)(a) is the distribution over consequences determined by the state and action profile of a period, defined also at unplayed profiles and unvisited states. Never accessed by agents. Not to be confused with: On-path conditionals, which mix mechanisms according to which states co-occur with which play.
- **causal equivalence; causal-equivalence partition** — `s ~ s' iff tau(s) = tau(s')`. Two states are causally equivalent if the objective assignment gives them the same mechanism; P^tau is the partition of S into causal-equivalence classes. Not to be confused with: Observational equivalence of two worlds (agreement of consequence laws at reachable state-profile pairs).
- **awareness partition** — `P_i in Pi(S)`. Agent i's partition P_i of S. Theories and policies are defined on its cells, conjectures condition only on its cells, and beliefs range only over theories expressible on it. An awareness cell is an implicit causal claim that the states it pools are alike in how actions produce consequences. Not to be confused with: An information partition about which state occurred, or inattention: the agent perceives its current cell, but awareness bounds which distinctions it can represent at all.
- **expressible theories** — `M(P_i) = {m : P_i -> Theta}`. The cell-constant mechanism assignments on the awareness partition; theory m predicts x_i ~ marg_i m(C)(a) at cell C under profile a. Restricting theories to a single mechanism per cell is substantive and deliberate. Not to be confused with: Arbitrary conditional distributions per cell (a saturated model space that no experience could reject); conjectures about rival play.
- **sufficient partition** — `P refines P^tau'`. Partition P is sufficient for an assignment tau' if tau' is constant on every cell of P, equivalently if P refines P^tau'. The objective assignment is expressible for agent i iff P_i is sufficient for tau. Not to be confused with: Statistical sufficiency.
- **conjecture** — `q_i : P_i -> Delta(A_-i)`. Agent i's strategic claim about rival play, conditioning on its own awareness cells and updated empirically from observed play. Not to be confused with: A theory, which is a causal claim about the objective system; the diagnostic apparatus tests theories, not conjectures.
- **deliberative record** — `r_i = multiset of (C_{P_i}(s), a, x_i)`. The multiset r_i of retained tokens (C_{P_i}(s), a, x_i); the only record on which the agent's beliefs, tests and decisions may depend. Not to be confused with: The analyst record r_bar_i, which additionally carries the underlying state of each token.
- **analyst record** — `r_bar_i = multiset of (s, C_{P_i}(s), a, x_i)`. The record r_bar_i that additionally carries the underlying state s of each token. Used only by analyst-level constructions (restorative correspondence, irreparability, warrant accounting) and consumed by the representation-producing technology; never by deliberation. Not to be confused with: Anything the agent can deliberate on.
- **recodability** On acquiring a refinement P' the agent can classify every retained token under P', producing a recoded deliberative record r_i^{P'}; recoding is total and infallible once the predicate is held and unavailable before. Not to be confused with: Pre-insight access to the distinguishing predicate.
- **standing policy; belief-optimal policy** — `sigma_i_star(C) in argmax_{a_i} sum_m mu_i(m) sum_{a_-i} q_i(a_-i|C) E_{x~m(C)(a_i,a_-i)}[pi_i(x_i)]`. The standing policy sigma_i is carried from one period to the next. The belief-optimal policy plays, at each cell, an action maximizing subjective expected payoff under the acting belief and conjecture. It is undefined when the acting belief is undefined, and then the standing policy is carried forward. Not to be confused with: The policy actually played, which changes only when the switching hurdle is cleared.
- **subjective per-period value** — `U_i(sigma|mu_i,q_i) = sum_C rho(C) sum_m mu_i(m) sum_{a_-i} q_i(a_-i|C) E_{x~m(C)(sigma(C),a_-i)}[pi_i(x_i)], rho(C) = sum_{s in C} rho(s)`. Agent i's subjective per-period value of a policy given its acting belief and conjecture, weighting cells by their known probabilities. Not to be confused with: Objective expected payoff.
- **valuation horizon; switching hurdle; policy inertia** — `switch iff T*(U_i(sigma_i_star|mu_i,q_i) - U_i(sigma_i|mu_i,q_i)) > kappa_i`. A lumpy one-time cost is valued against the gain it produces over a common undiscounted horizon of T periods. The agent replaces sigma_i by sigma_i_star iff the horizon-T gain exceeds the one-time switching hurdle kappa_i >= 0; the inaction band is the source of policy inertia, which is an outcome rather than an assumption. Not to be confused with: Discounting (T is a bounded-rationality stand-in for it).
- **on-path conditional** — `p_i*(.|C,a) = sum_{s in S(C,a)} rho(s|S(C,a)) * marg_i tau(s)(a), S(C,a) = {s in C : a(s) = a}`. Over a stable stretch with constant standing profile, agent i's distribution of own consequences at an observation pair (C,a): a selection object mixing the mechanisms of the states in C that receive profile a. Not to be confused with: Any mechanism's own conditional.
- **exact calibration** — `eta = 0, w(n) = 0 for all n`. Deterministic mechanisms with adequacy tolerance eta = 0 and finite-sample slack w = 0, in which tests reject at the first impossible observation and false alarms are zero; the running example uses it. Not to be confused with: The general stochastic case, in which every definition is stated.
- **adequate; eta-adequate; eta-inadequate** — `TV(p_i*(.|C,a), marg_i m(C)(a)) <= eta`. Theory m is adequate at a visited pair (C,a) when the total-variation distance between the on-path conditional and m's prediction is at most eta. Under a standing profile, m is eta-adequate if it is adequate at every visited pair and eta-inadequate if it is inadequate at some visited pair. Not to be confused with: Objective truth (an adequate theory can be false at pooled states or unvisited profiles); retrospective fit to a fixed record.
- **fit (retrospective)** — `TV(p_hat_i(.|C,a), marg_i m(C)(a)) <= eta + w(n_i(C,a))`. Scoring a candidate against a fixed record rather than a stream, using counts n_i(C,a), empirical conditionals p_hat_i, and a nonnegative, nonincreasing slack w(.) -> 0. Not to be confused with: Sequential adequacy testing.
- **evidence process; dissonance capacity** — `reject m when log(E_i^m) >= b_i; Pr(sup_t E_i^m(t) >= exp(b_i)) <= exp(-b_i)`. For each admitted theory, a nonnegative process E_i^m initialized at 1. It is a supermartingale under adequacy and diverges almost surely under recurrent strict inadequacy (Appendix A betting mixture; a zero-likelihood detector in the deterministic case). The theory is rejected permanently at the first period with log E_i^m >= b_i, and the capacity b_i > 0 is the agent's evidence threshold for abandoning a theory. Not to be confused with: The Bayesian posterior: the reference posterior is never conditioned on the test.
- **active set** — `M_hat_i(P_i, r_i)`. The theories admitted when the current language was initialized that have not subsequently been rejected by the sequential test. It only shrinks, and rejected theories never re-enter. Not to be confused with: The expressible set M(P_i); the support of the posterior.
- **judged updating (reference posterior and acting belief)** — `mu_i(m) = mu_bar_i(m) / sum_{m~ in M_hat_i} mu_bar_i(m~) for m in M_hat_i`. Beliefs have two tiers. The reference posterior mu_bar_i starts at a full-support prior and updates by token likelihoods, never conditioned on the test. The acting belief mu_i is the reference posterior restricted and renormalized to the current active set, and it is undefined when the active set is empty. Not to be confused with: Ordinary Bayesian conditioning, with which it coincides only in the deterministic case while the normalizer is positive.
- **causal dissonance** — `M_hat_i(P_i, r_i) = {}`. Agent i experiences causal dissonance at any period at which its active set is empty: no theory admitted on the current language remains unrejected. Dissonance poses the question whether an unrepresented distinction among the pooled states can reconcile the record, and if so what it is. Not to be confused with: A low posterior or surprise (posterior mass always sums to one); proof of objective representational insufficiency, which holds only under exact deterministic testing.
- **exposed; concealing** Under a standing profile, awareness P_i is exposed if every expressible theory is eta-inadequate. A standing profile is concealing for agent i if some expressible theory is eta-adequate under it while false as a claim about tau at some state its cells cover. Not to be confused with: Dissonance itself: exposure yields dissonance almost surely only under the activity proviso of Proposition 6.5.
- **repair test; repair-consistent active set** — `M_hat_i^R(P', r_bar_i) = {m' in M(P') : TV(p_hat_i(.|C',a), marg_i m'(C')(a)) <= eta + w(n_i(C',a)) for every (C',a) with n_i(C',a) > 0}`. For a candidate refinement P', the theories on P' that fit the recoded analyst record at every nonempty recoded cell-profile pair. Refinement can reconcile observations but cannot delete their constraints. Not to be confused with: The deliberative grouping test, which applies only where counts are at least n_bar.
- **restorative; minimally restorative refinement** — `restorative iff M_hat_i^R(P', r_bar_i) != {}`. A strict refinement P' of P_i is restorative if its repair-consistent active set is nonempty, and minimally restorative if no restorative partition lies strictly between P_i and P'. R(P_i, r_bar_i) is the set of minimally restorative refinements. This is an analyst-level object. Not to be confused with: An objectively correct refinement.
- **irreparable record** — `R(P_i, r_bar_i) = {}`. A dissonant record with no minimally restorative refinement. It is possible only with stochastic mechanisms, it is analyst-level, and the agent cannot in general tell it from a repairable record. Not to be confused with: A record whose repair is merely unpriced or abandoned.
- **representation-producing technology; truth-blind** — `phi_i : (P_i, r_bar_i) -> {P_i} union R(P_i, r_bar_i)`. A possibly randomized map from the analyst record to the current partition (failure) or to a minimally restorative refinement (success). It is truth-blind: its output distribution depends only on (P_i, r_bar_i), never directly on tau. A complexity ordering on refinements may restrict its range. Not to be confused with: A model of the semantic creative act (treated as a primitive); a pre-insight menu the agent can name, price, or condition on.
- **formulation rule (record-measurable and truth-blind)** — `psi_i(. | P_i', r_i^{P_i'}) in Delta(M_hat_i^R(P_i', r_bar_i))`. A possibly randomized kernel selecting a theory from the repair-consistent active set on the refined language. It is record-measurable and truth-blind if it depends only on its displayed arguments and on internal randomness independent of the objective assignment and all draws; a truth-blind inquiry-and-formulation rule is such a pair (phi_i, psi_i). Not to be confused with: Transmission from an informed source, which can be correlated with the objective assignment.
- **bridge prior** — `mu_i^+ in Delta(M(P_i'))`. A fresh full-support prior on the expanded language, formed after successful production, from which ordinary updating resumes; Bayesian coherence does not determine it from the pre-dissonance belief. Not to be confused with: A Bayesian update of the old prior (the domains differ).
- **costly, elective inquiry** — `cost gamma_i > 0 per election`. The technology operates only when agent i is dissonant and elects inquiry, at cost gamma_i > 0 in payoff units. On failure or abstention the standing policy is carried forward, and acquired distinctions are never lost. Not to be confused with: Continuous search or experimentation within a fixed language.
- **delabeled achievability weight** — `lambda_i^(k) in (0,1], nonincreasing in k, lambda_i^(k) -> 0`. A subjective probability that an election produces a refinement delivering the estimated leverage. It is defined on that event alone, not on the identities of possible outputs, and is nonincreasing in the count k of consecutive failed elections, tending to 0. It is awareness of unawareness in delabeled form. Not to be confused with: A prior over named candidate refinements; a mere success probability.
- **deliberative grouping; coherent group** — `Theta_i(g) = {theta in Theta : TV(p_hat_g(.|a), marg_i theta(a)) <= eta + w(n_g(a)) for every a with n_g(a) >= n_bar}`. A deliberative grouping G is a partition of the record's tokens. Theta_i(g) is the set of mechanisms that pass the grouping test on group g wherever the count is at least n_bar. A group is coherent if Theta_i(g) is nonempty, and a grouping is coherent if all its groups are; G_i(r_i) is the finite family of coherent groupings. It uses no state labels. Not to be confused with: A partition of S or a candidate refinement: a coherent grouping is the agent's hypothesis about which particulars share a mechanism and need not be realizable.
- **per-token robust gain** — `Delta_i^G(t,a_i) = min_{a_-i} min_{theta in Theta_i(g(t))} (E_{x~theta(a_i,a_-i)}[pi_i(x_i)] - E_{x~theta(sigma_i(t),a_-i)}[pi_i(x_i)])`. The worst case, over rival responses and over the mechanisms the group's record leaves open, of the payoff gain from proposing action a_i rather than the standing action at the token's cell. Not to be confused with: An expected gain under a belief.
- **robust fine-contingent value; current-language benchmark; gross representational increment** — `Lfine = sum_C sum_g max_{a_i} sum_{t in r_i(C,g)} Delta_i^G(t,a_i); Lcur = sum_C max_{a_i} sum_g sum_{t in r_i(C,g)} Delta_i^G(t,a_i); J = Lfine - Lcur`. L_fine lets action vary by each nonempty current-cell/group intersection. L_cur requires one action per current awareness cell, evaluated under the same coherent grouping. J is their difference. Not to be confused with: A new dissonant policy-switching rule: L_cur is a counterfactual valuation benchmark.
- **deliberative leverage (value of a question)** — `B = (T/r)*max({0} union {J(G) : G in G_i(r_i)}) if M_hat_i(P_i,r_i) = {}, else 0; for the best grouping B = (T/r)*(Lfine - Lcur)`. At a dissonant record, T over the record size times the largest gross representational increment over coherent groupings, with outside option 0; zero when the active set is nonempty. It prices the incremental gross action value of making a coherent grouping actionable, on the premise that the empirical token distribution recurs. Not to be confused with: Value of information or uncertainty reduction (it prices action separation); a net expected payoff after switching costs; an expected value over named candidate answers.
- **pointwise transparency** — `|Theta_i({t})| = 1 for every retained token t`. In the deterministic zero-tolerance case with n_bar = 1 and w = 0, a record is pointwise transparent if every retained token pins a single mechanism. The leverage estimate is then exact and repair-invariant. Not to be confused with: Determinism alone, which certifies separability of conflicting tokens but does not make every grouping realizable.
- **representation-relative coherence** — `Coh_i(O_i) = mu_i^+(m') / sum_{m~ in M_hat_i^R(P_i', r_bar_i)} mu_i^+(m~)`. The probability that the initial acting belief on the refined language (the bridge prior restricted and renormalized to the repair-consistent active set) assigns to the formulated theory. Not to be confused with: Selection-adjusted warrant.
- **reflective assessment problem** An analyst-level pair (calT, nu): a finite set of candidate objective assignments and a full-support reflective prior. Conditioning uses the analyst record and the complete pre-production inquiry history h_i. Not to be confused with: The agent's own bridge prior or acting belief.
- **selection-adjusted warrant** — `W(o) = nu(A_o | r_bar_i, h_i, O_i = o), A_o = {tau' in calT : tau'(s) = m_o(C_{P'}(s)) for every s}`. The reflective probability, given the analyst record, the inquiry history and the realized output, that the formulated theory is objectively correct, relative to the specified assessment problem. Not to be confused with: Representation-relative coherence (the agent's confidence); retrospective fit.
- **signed warrant gap** — `gap = Coh - W`. Coherence minus warrant at a realized output; its sign is unrestricted in general. Not to be confused with: An error of the agent's updating.
- **self-confirming unawareness equilibrium (SCUE)** — `(i) T*(U_i(sigma_i_star|mu_i,q_i) - U_i(sigma_i|mu_i,q_i)) <= kappa_i; (iv) lambda_i^(k_i)*B_hat_i(r_i) < gamma_i`. A profile of epistemic states (P_i, r_i, mu_bar_i, q_i, sigma_i, k_i) such that, under the play it induces: (i) no switch clears the hurdle; (ii) conjectures agree in the limit with induced play at on-path cells; (iii) each active set is almost surely eventually nonempty and acting-belief mass on eta-inadequate theories vanishes; (iv) no agent elects inquiry. Not to be confused with: A self-confirming equilibrium of the fixed-language tradition (SCUE adds language survival and no priced question); convergence of play.
- **partial SCUE** A configuration in which the SCUE conditions hold for every non-stasis agent, with the stasis agents' constant carried-forward policies taken as fixed environment. Not to be confused with: A full SCUE.
- **dissonant stasis; absorbing profile** — `lambda_i^(k_i)*B_hat_i(r_i) < gamma_i`. An agent is in dissonant stasis from some period on if dissonance recurs while it never both elects and succeeds: either its question is unpriced (zero leverage, an abandoned question, possibly at an irreparable record) or elected production always fails. It carries its standing policy with a standing anomaly. A population profile is absorbing if every agent is eventually either in a SCUE role (never dissonant) or in dissonant stasis. Not to be confused with: SCUE, in which a represented model class survives.
- **enlightened inertia** — `T*(U_i(sigma_i_star) - U_i(sigma_i)) <= kappa_i after refinement`. Having acquired a refinement and an improved model, the agent carries its old policy forward because the realized horizon-T gain of the recommended switch does not clear the switching hurdle. Not to be confused with: Ignorance or dissonant stasis.
- **abandoned question; rational anomaly tolerance** — `lambda_i^(k)*B_hat_i < gamma_i`. An abandoned question is a repairable, positively valued defect that the agent stops pursuing because repeated failure has driven lambda_i^(k)*B_hat_i below cost. Anomaly tolerance is rational when the agent's coherent groupings carry no gross representational leverage. Not to be confused with: Indifference or a pathology of the model.
- **payoff-relevant policy error** — `E_{x~tau(s)(a^sigma(s))}[pi_i(x_i)] < max_{a_i~} E_{x~tau(s)(a_i~, a_-i^sigma(s))}[pi_i(x_i)]`. At state s the standing action is strictly suboptimal under objective evaluation, given the rivals' induced actions. Not to be confused with: A change in the set of optimal actions that leaves the standing action optimal.
- **diagnostic at tolerance eta; strictly punishing** — `min_{m in M(P_i)} TV(p_i*(.|C_{P_i}(s), a^sigma(s)), marg_i m(C_{P_i}(s))(a^sigma(s))) > eta`. Primitives are diagnostic if, for every profile satisfying SCUE conditions (i)-(ii), any payoff-relevant policy error implies the erring agent's awareness is exposed. They are strictly punishing if at every payoff-relevant policy error the on-path conditional is more than eta from every expressible prediction. Not to be confused with: Global openness of diagnosticity (only local robustness of strict margins is claimed).
- **insight** — `P_i -> P_i' with P_i' strictly refining P_i`. Represented formally as the production of a distinction, a refinement of the awareness partition; the model explains when insight is demanded, what constrains it, what it is worth and what its output warrants. With the mechanism vocabulary fixed, insight means discovering which differences matter. Not to be confused with: The conscious creative or semantic act (a primitive); expansion of the mechanism vocabulary.
- **novelty (agent-relative)** Refinement makes an analyst-represented distinction available to the agent but does not create objective states or elementary mechanisms. Not to be confused with: Creation of the analyst's ontology or a new kind of causal mechanism.
- **row law; column world; demand parameter beta (running example)** — `tau(s_rc) = theta^r; tau'(s_rc) = theta^c; pooled payoff on the wrong architecture = beta/2`. In Example 1, the objective assignment is the row law (the row coordinate is causally relevant); the column world is the alternative assignment. Under theta^a, differentiated play gives payoff 1 to the firm offering a and 0 to its rival; pooling on a gives 1/2 each; pooling on the other architecture gives beta/2 each. Under theta^n each receives 1/2. Not to be confused with: General results; the numbers are example-specific.
