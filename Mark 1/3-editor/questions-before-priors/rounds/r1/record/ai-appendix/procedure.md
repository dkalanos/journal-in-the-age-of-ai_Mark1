# Procedure

1. Fix the primitives: agents N = {1, ..., n}; a finite state space S with full-support rho; finite action sets A_i, A = prod A_i; finite consequence spaces X_i with payoffs u_i: X_i -> R; a finite mechanism vocabulary Theta of maps theta: A -> Delta(X); and the objective assignment tau: S -> Theta, giving F(. | s, a) = tau(s)(a). Time runs in generations of T episodes; states are drawn i.i.d. each episode.
2. For each agent fix the awareness partition P_i and form the expressible theories M(P_i) = {m : P_i -> Theta}; fix a full-support reference prior mubar_i on M(P_i) and a P_i-measurable conjecture q_i: P_i -> Delta(A_-i).
3. At the start of each generation compute the acting belief mu_i (mubar_i restricted and renormalized to the current active set) and choose the committed policy sigma_i cell by cell from equation (3): sigma_i(C) maximizes sum_m mu_i(m) sum_{a_-i} q_i(a_-i | C) E_{x ~ m(C)(a_i, a_-i)}[u_i(x_i)]. If the active set is empty and no new representation has been produced, carry the previous policy forward. Hold the policy for T episodes.
4. From the committed profile sigma derive the deterministic profile a(s) = (sigma_j(C_{P_j}(s)))_j at each state, the visited sets S(C, a) = {s in C : a(s) = a}, and the on-path conditional p_i*(. | C, a) = sum_{s in S(C, a)} rho(s | S(C, a)) marg_i tau(s)(a) (equation (4)).
5. At the end of each generation record the tokens (C_{P_i}(s), a, x_i), compute counts n_i(C, a) and empirical conditionals phat_i(. | C, a), and apply the test of equation (5) to each m in M(P_i): m passes if TV(phat_i(. | C, a), marg_i m(C)(a)) <= eta + w(n_i(C, a)) at every pair with n_i(C, a) >= nbar. The active set is the set of passing theories. Update the reference posterior by likelihoods alone. In the deterministic instance (eta = 0, nbar = 1, w = 0) passing is exact consistency with every retained token.
6. Declare causal dissonance when the active set is empty. To decide whether dissonance will occur under a fixed profile, check exposure: P_i is exposed if every m in M(P_i) is eta-inadequate, i.e. TV(p_i*(. | C, a), marg_i m(C)(a)) > eta at some visited pair; under the genericity hypothesis (no distance equals eta exactly) Lemma 5.2 gives almost-sure eventual acceptance of adequate and rejection of inadequate theories, and Proposition 5.5 gives dissonance from some generation onward almost surely iff P_i is exposed.
7. At a dissonant record enumerate the strict refinements P' of P_i. Recode the tokens to P' using the analyst-level states. A cell is coherent if some single mechanism passes the test on its recoded tokens; by Proposition 6.2 the recoded active set is nonempty iff every cell is coherent, so P' is restorative iff every cell is coherent. R(P_i, r_i) is the set of restorative refinements with no restorative refinement strictly between P_i and them. If R is empty the record is irreparable: B_i = 0 and the technology fails surely.
8. For nonidentification compare two assignments tau and tau' that induce the same record distribution under the played profiles: any truth-blind rule has the same output distribution in both worlds, and if their eta-adequate sets at the visited pairs are disjoint no truth-blind rule is adequate with probability one in both (Theorem 6.5). A partition sufficient for every assignment in a candidate set T must refine the join over T of the causal-equivalence partitions P^tau' (Proposition 6.6).
9. Compute leverage. For each token t let Theta_i(t) be the set of mechanisms that some repair P' in R, through a theory in its recoded active set, assigns to t's cell. Form the token partition each P' induces and take their meet Lambda_i(r_i). Maximize equation (6) over Lambda_i-measurable rules psi: B_i(r_i) = max_psi sum_t min_{a_-i} min_{theta in Theta_i(t)} ( E_{x ~ theta(psi(t), a_-i)}[u_i(x_i)] - E_{x ~ theta(sigma_i(t), a_-i)}[u_i(x_i)] ). psi = sigma_i is feasible, so B_i >= 0. The agent elects inquiry iff lambda_i B_i(r_i) >= gamma_i (Proposition 7.4).
10. On an elected and successful production the technology returns some P' in R (possibly restricted by kappa). Formulation selects m' in Mhat_i(P', r_i) on the recoded record; a full-support bridge prior mu_i^+ on M(P') is formed; both belief tiers restart from it; representation-relative coherence is mu_i^+ renormalized on the active set, evaluated at m'.
11. Compute warrant. Choose a reflective assessment (T, nu). The posterior on tau' in T is proportional to nu(tau') times the record's likelihood under tau' times the probability of the production event given the record; under truth blindness the last factor is constant across T and cancels, and post-restoration fit has likelihood one, so W equals the ordinary reflective posterior on the record (Theorem 8.2); among candidates with equal record likelihood W equals the prior. The warrant gap is G = Coh(m') - W. The agent can express the assessment only if P_i refines the join of the candidates' partitions (Corollary 8.3).
12. For the dynamics (Theorem 9.4) bound the total cell count sum_i |P_i|, an integer in [n, n|S|] that strictly increases at every successful production: at most n(|S| - 1) refinements occur. Enumerate elections with success indicators Z_k; each election occurs at a dissonant record with R nonempty, so its conditional success probability is at least pi_lower, and by Levy's extension of the Borel-Cantelli lemma infinitely many elections would give infinitely many successes, which the cell-count bound forbids: finitely many elections. After G*, the larger of the last refinement and last election generation, no language changes and no cost is paid. On the event of an eventually constant profile sigma*, frozen pairs' constraints are eventually constant, recurrent pairs' empirical conditionals converge to p_i* under sigma*, generic eta makes each constraint eventually permanently satisfied or failed, so each active set is eventually constant and eta-adequate at recurrent pairs; a nonempty terminal active set means never dissonant, an empty one means dissonant stasis with lambda_i B_i < gamma_i.
13. To verify a SCUE (Definition 9.1) check, under the induced play: (i) each sigma_i solves equation (3); (ii) each q_i agrees with induced play at every on-path cell; (iii) each active set is almost surely eventually nonempty and each acting belief is supported on eta-adequate theories; (iv) lambda_i B_i(r_i) < gamma_i along the induced records. Proposition 9.5(i): an almost surely converging configuration limits, off the agents in dissonant stasis, to a SCUE. Proposition 9.5(ii): a SCUE whose play generates only B_i = 0 records, with strict optimality under every conjecture and active theory, persists surely under deterministic mechanisms; under stochastic mechanisms, with every active theory eta'-adequate for some eta' < eta and strict optimality extended to (eta + epsilon)-adequate theories for some epsilon in (0, 2(eta - eta')], choose N(delta) so that w(n) <= epsilon/2 for n >= N and sum_{n >= N} K exp(-n epsilon^2/2) <= delta/P (K the finite-alphabet Hoeffding constant, P the number of recurrent pairs); then with probability at least 1 - delta play never changes and the SCUE conditions hold thereafter.
14. For where error lives (Propositions 9.8 and 9.11) use condition (iii): surviving theories are eta-adequate at visited pairs, so falsity hides only at pooled states whose mixture imitates a single mechanism at the visited profiles and at unvisited profiles. If the environment is diagnostic (every payoff-relevantly false theory is eta-inadequate under every SCUE-sustainable profile) no SCUE carries payoff-relevant error; otherwise such error requires a concealing profile to be sustained.
15. Running example, all exact (eta = 0, nbar = 1, w = 0): S = {s00, s01, s10, s11} with uniform rho; A_i = {0, 1}; Theta = {theta^0, theta^1, theta^n}; under theta^a, differentiated firms give 1 to the firm offering a and 0 to its rival, pooling on a gives 1/2 each, pooling on the other architecture gives beta/2 each; under theta^n each firm gets 1/2; x_i = (own payoff, observed action profile); tau(s_rc) = theta^r; both firms start at P_i^0 = {S}. Steps: (a) dominance: with p^a the belief mass on theories assigning theta^a at the cell, the gain of action 0 over 1 is p^0/2 - (1 - beta/2) p^1 against a rival playing 0 and (1 - beta/2) p^0 - p^1/2 against a rival playing 1; action 0 is strictly dominant iff p^0 > (2 - beta) p^1, at beta = 1 simple belief asymmetry. (b) discovery: firm 1 on 0, firm 2 on 1, s00 and s11 served; firm 1 gets 1 at s00 and 0 at s11 (firm 2 the reverse); m^0, m^1, m^n each fail one episode, so both active sets empty. Pooled play would give 1/2 at every episode and pass all three theories (concealing). (c) repairs: 15 partitions of four states; those keeping s00 and s11 together are the 5 partitions of three elements, so 10 separate them; the minimal ones are the row partition, the column partition and the two isolating one labeled state (4), and 15 - 4 = 11 are excluded. The column world tau'(s_rc) = theta^c reproduces the record; a rule formulating the row refinement with probability q succeeds with q in the row world and 1 - q in the column world, worst case min(q, 1 - q) <= 1/2; the join of the row and column partitions is the four singletons. (d) leverage: each firm's committed action disagrees with one revealed label; implementing it at the recurrence gains 1/2 - 0 if the rival plays the label and 1 - 1/2 if the rival plays the committed action, so B_i = 1/2 and the firm elects iff lambda_i/2 >= gamma_i. Validation (firm 1 row-refined on the row law, firm 2 column-refined on the column law, all four states served on top of the retained record): B_1 = 0, B_2 = 2 x 1/2 = 1. (e) warrant: the column (or row) refinement pins a unique theory, coherence 1; with T = {row world, column world} and nu = 1/2, equal record likelihoods give W = 1/2 and G = 1/2; a differentiated off-diagonal observation makes the reflective posterior degenerate. (f) trap: both column-refined and pooled on the column policy; at beta = 1 every payoff is 1/2, equal to the column theory's prediction, active sets never empty, B_i = 0: a SCUE with payoff-relevant error. For beta < 1 an off-diagonal state pays beta/2 against a prediction of 1/2 (shortfall (1 - beta)/2, destroyed value 1 - beta): dissonance, positive leverage and a degenerate reflective posterior. (g) persistence: firm 1 on the row law, firm 2 constant: per-episode payoffs (1/2 + 1/2 + 1 + 1)/4 = 3/4 and (1/2 + 1/2 + 0 + 0)/4 = 1/4; firm 2's record rejects all three constant theories; stasis whenever gamma_2 > lambda_2 B_2.
